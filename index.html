Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how US Citizenship Test 2026 Prep handles user information.

Government Non-Affiliation Disclaimer:
This application is an independent educational practice resource and is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), Department of Homeland Security (DHS), or any government entity. All civics study questions are public domain materials from uscis.gov.

1. 100% Offline-First (Zero Personal Data Collected)

We respect your privacy. The app does not collect, record, track, transmit, or sell any personal data (such as names, emails, addresses, location data, or immigration documents). All exam history, mistake tracking, and state selections remain 100% private on your own device.

2. Audio Features (Native TTS)

Question and answer audio is generated locally on your device via Android's built-in Text-to-Speech engine. No voice recordings or audio transmissions are ever made.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide free access, the app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-identifying telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Users can disable all ads at any time via in-app settings.

4. Contact

For questions or assistance regarding this application or privacy policy, please reach out to our developer support team.
